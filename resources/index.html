CTSNL

The Computer Technology Society Newfoundland Labrador generally known as CTSNL, is a community group for people who work in or have an interest in software development, technology, and computing in Newfoundland and Labrador.

CTSNL now uses Discord for community conversations and questions. Visit ctsnl.ca for its Discord join link and meetup information.

Silicon Harbour

Silicon Harbour lists tech events, jobs, and companies in St. John’s and Newfoundland and Labrador.
